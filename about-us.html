ABOUT Y E F

Yerwa Empowerment Foundation (YEF) is a non-governmental not-for-profit organization founded in late 2019 aimed to respond to humanitarian crisis by empowering Internally Displaced Persons (women and children) in formal/informal camp settings and host communities through Education, Skills Acquisition, Capacity Building and Sanitation and Hygiene promotion..

Our developmental projects include the creation of an IT based environment for our beneficiaries. YEF also supports some educational institutions across Maiduguri, Jere, Konduga and Mafa Local Government Areas. The foundation consists of professionals from various disciplines that are dedicated in taking it to a greater height. YEF has no share-holders, no dividends and no profit motive..
